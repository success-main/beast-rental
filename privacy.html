Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09 September 2026. This template should be customized for your business and reviewed against applicable Nigerian data protection requirements (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act) before launch.

Information We Collect

  • Name, email address and phone number
  • Shop or farm rental information (type and unit number)
  • Account information (serial number, role, status)
  • Payment records (amount, currency, Paystack reference, transaction ID, status — never full card details)
  • Transaction and rental history
  • QR verification records and events
  • Basic technical/security information necessary to protect your account (e.g. sign-in timestamps)

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to create and manage your account, process and verify rental payments, generate receipts and QR codes, send rental-related notifications, and maintain the security and integrity of the Platform.

How We Store Your Information

Information is stored using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, both provided by Google. Passwords are managed entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in our database in plain text or otherwise accessible form.

Security

We apply Firestore Security Rules, Firebase Authentication, and server-side verification through Cloud Functions to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we continuously work to improve our safeguards.

Access to Your Information

You can access and update most of your profile information from your dashboard. Certain fields — such as your serial number and receipt records — are read-only to preserve the integrity of the rental system.

Data Retention

We retain account, rental, payment and receipt records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward as required for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please reach out via the Contact page.
