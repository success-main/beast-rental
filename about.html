About Us

Built to make rentals simple & secure

Beast Rentals exists to remove the friction, paperwork and uncertainty from shop and farm rental management.

About Beast Rentals

Beast Rentals is a professional rental-management platform built for landlords and tenants of shop and farm units. It brings registration, annual payments, digital receipts and QR-based verification into a single, secure dashboard experience.

Our Mission

To give every shop and farm tenant a transparent, verifiable record of their rental — and to give administrators the tools to manage that process efficiently and securely.

Our Vision

A rental ecosystem where every payment is verifiable, every receipt is authentic, and every rental status is a tap away — with fraud designed out from the start.

What We Offer

Shop Rental Management

Register, pay for and track individual shop units with a dedicated dashboard.

Farm Rental Management

The same secure workflow, tailored for farm plot tenants.

Secure Payments

Every annual payment is verified server-side through Paystack before activation.

Digital Receipts

Unique, downloadable receipts generated automatically after verification.

QR Verification

Instant authenticity checks via secure, revocable QR tokens.

Rental Countdown

A live, color-coded countdown so no one is caught off guard by expiry.
